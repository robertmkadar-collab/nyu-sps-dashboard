Enrollment Funnel
With days until classes begin, the upper funnel is outperforming Fall '25 across the board — started applications are up 15%, completions up 8%, and admits up 9%. However, deposits and registrations are trailing last year, sitting at 52 and 38 respectively against a 75-seat goal. The conversion rate from admitted to deposited is 58%, below last cycle's 71%. The priority for the remaining weeks is clear: yield, not lead generation — every admitted student who doesn't deposit represents the most expensive lost opportunity in the funnel.
